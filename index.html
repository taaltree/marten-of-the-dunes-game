Marten of the Dunes

You are a Humboldt marten — a threatened, one-kilogram carnivore living in a narrow strip of shore-pine forest between the Pacific and the open sand. Keep your energy up by hunting the prey real martens eat and stripping huckleberry and salal. Avoid bobcats by slipping into dense shrubs, and think twice before crossing open sand where off-highway vehicles roam.

This game needs a keyboard and mouse — open it on a laptop or desktop computer.

Controls

W A S D — trot · Shift bound · C crouch-stalk · Z walk
Mouse — look and aim. The reticle locks onto prey in pounce range.
Click — pounce. Hold to coil for a longer leap; aim up to snatch birds from shrubs.
E — eat a catch, a cached carcass, or ripe berries (hold position).
Space — jump. Next to a trunk: climb. W/S up/down, A/D around. Look along a limb and press W to walk out on it.
Q or right mouse — sense: animals within ~35 m show through the leaves in their own colours with an amber rim (predators red), visible ones get a thin outline, voles and mice leave scent trails, ripe fruit is marked.
R — track the animal in your sights: its marker, distance and alertness bar stay on screen (an arrow points to it when it is off screen), a close-up of it appears at the bottom right, and the leaves between you thin out. Press R again to switch or stop.
Listen — ♪ marks a bird singing nearby (with its name up close), ( ) ripples mark something rustling in the litter, and a dashed bracket marks an animal you can see. Fruiting huckleberry and salal show black and purple berries; stand next to them and press E.
F — stand on hind legs to scan: the view narrows and you spot animals farther off.
V — nose-cam (first person) · M — map · Tab — field guide
How to hunt — 1. Listen (♪, rustles) or hold Q: hidden animals show as amber shapes. 2. R tracks one. 3. C crouch and creep closer while it feeds (head down); stop while it watches (head up): moving in its view alarms it. 4. Within about 3.5 m the bracket turns solid: green = it hasn't noticed you, amber = it's watching, red = alarmed. Click to pounce (a click leaps ~2.5 m; hold to coil for up to ~4 m and release when the prompt says so). An alert animal may dodge. Your marten eats as soon as it lands.
Survival tips — bobcats outrun you in the open but can barely push through dense huckleberry/salal; climb high if one corners you. OHVs can't see you: listen for engines and watch for orange whip flags over the dune crests.

Press M to close. Aerial: USDA NAIP. 3.0 × 2.5 km around the Horsfall dune lakes, southern Oregon Dunes NRA.
